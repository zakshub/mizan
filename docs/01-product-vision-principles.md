# 01. Product Vision and Principles

## What the product is
- A structured multi-agent deliberation platform.
- A user supplies a decision topic and context, then watches thesis, antithesis, evidence, and judge roles work in one channel.
- The output is a decision record, not a summary.

## Brand and positioning
- Primary brand: Mizan.
- English descriptor: Decision Room.
- Tagline: Two perspectives. One clearer decision.
- Initial workspace: Career and Hiring.
- Default theme: Light.
- Keep the engine domain-agnostic so later expert packs are possible.

## Core principles
- No fake expertise.
- Bounded debate.
- Evidence before authority.
- User stays in control.
- Decision, not theatre.
- Provider independence.
- Language integrity.
- Traceability.

## Primary user problem
- Current multi-model comparison is manual, fragmented, and loses context.
- The product should keep the full debate inside one live channel.
